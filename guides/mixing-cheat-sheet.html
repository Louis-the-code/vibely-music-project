Mixing Cheat Sheet

Stems + vocals in FL Studio 21 · your Stem Mixer template, master presets, delay presets and 18 vocal presets

1 · How your mix flows

Balance the song with the 4 bus faders. Use single tracks only to fix one sound. Vocal presets go on tracks 15-16 (or any track).

2 · Starting levels (peak meter, before the master chain)

The 3 rules that matter
  1. Master peaks around -6 to -3 dB before the mastering chain. Never in the red while mixing.
  2. Lead vocal sits 3-6 dB above the loudest instrument. It's the star.
  3. Backing = 6-9 dB under the lead. Adlibs = 4-6 dB under. Doubles: 4-6 dB under.

Balancing order

  1. Kick alone → set it.
  2. Add 808 / bass until it feels equal to the kick by ear.
  3. Snare/clap, then hats/perc.
  4. Lead vocal on top.
  5. Chords → melodies → FX, tucked under the vocal.
  6. Switch to mono for 10 s: does the vocal and 808 stay solid?

Bars are starting points from common mix guides. Your ears decide. Level-match before judging "better".

3 · Frequency map: who lives where

Two things fighting in the same zone = mud. Fix by cutting one of them there (EQ), or ducking one under the other (sidechain), not by turning both up.

4 · Your 24 vocal presets: pick in 3 steps

Load one: Browser (left) → Mixer presets → drag e.g. VOX - Afrobeats - Female Lead onto your vocal's mixer track. Or right-click the track name → File → pick it. The full 8-plugin chain loads in one go.

Tweak these 3 things per song
  1. Compressor THRESHOLD (slots 4 and 5): sing/rap the loudest line and aim for 3-6 dB of turning down on slot 4 and 1-3 dB on slot 5. If your vocal is quiet, lower the threshold. If loud, raise it.
  2. De-esser: still hissy on "s"? Open Maximus and raise the mid band's compression. Lisping? Lower it.
  3. Pitcher (slot 1, OFF): for sung vocals, switch it ON and set the song's key. Leave it off for rap.

6 · Stereo stage: where things sit (seen from above)

Center: kick, snare, 808/bass (mono), lead vocal. Sides: perc, hats, harmonies, guitar, arps. Front ↔ back = less ↔ more reverb and high-end. Doubles: 30-50% L/R, harmonies 70-100% L/R (Rys Up Audio).

7 · "It sounds…" → fix it

ProblemTry this (in order)
Muddy / cloudyCut 250-450 Hz on chords/pads/vocal (-2 to -4 dB) · raise low-cuts on everything that isn't kick or bass · less reverb, and low-cut the reverb at 300 Hz

Vocal harsh / "sss"More de-essing (Maximus) · less presence/air boost · darker reverb (high cut 6-7k)
808 disappears on phoneFruity Soft Clipper on the 808 for harmonics · check kick vs 808 sidechain · don't low-cut the 808
Kick and 808 fightKick → Bass bus sidechain · or the 808 EQ trick (Peak Controller on kick, inverted link to an EQ band under 200 Hz on the 808)
Mix sounds small / narrowPan perc, hats and harmonies out · stereo chords/pads · keep low end centered
Vocal sounds far awayLess reverb/delay wet · shorter reverb · more pre-delay (30-40 ms) so the voice comes first
Pumping / breathingLess gain reduction on the master compressor · slower release on the sidechain
Distorts / red on masterPull the 4 bus faders down, not the master · master peaks around -6 dB before limiting
