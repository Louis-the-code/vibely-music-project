# Principles: AI-assisted, human-led

Vibely uses AI to remove friction, not to remove the artist.

1. **AI sets up, humans decide.** Routing, naming, preset building and maths are automated. Balance, tone, arrangement and "is this good?" stay human.
2. **Starting points, not answers.** Every preset is a researched default. You are expected to tweak it for the song, the voice and the moment.
3. **Show your sources.** Settings come from published mixing practice (sources in each doc), not from guesses.
4. **Stock tools first.** Everything runs on FL Studio's own plugins, so anyone with FL can use it.
5. **Never overwrite the creative.** The Stem Mixer script refuses to touch a project whose mixer you've already named. Presets load onto a track without renaming it.
6. **Test for real.** Tools are tested inside FL Studio, not just on paper, and failures are documented.
7. **Leave room for spontaneity.** Nothing here locks you in: every slot can be bypassed, swapped or deleted.
