# House

**Tempo:** 120 to 128 BPM · **Loudness target:** -9 to -8 LUFS

## What makes it sound like house
- Four-on-the-floor kick: the mix should "breathe" around it.
- Big, airy vocals that sit *in* the space, not dry on top.
- Echoes that swing against the grid (dotted 1/8) and long washes in breakdowns.

## Master chain (MASTER - House)
| Slot | Plugin | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fruity Parametric EQ 2 | FL preset *20Hz + 18kHz cut* |
| 2 | Fruity Compressor | 2.2:1, -8.4 dB thr, **33 ms attack** (kick punches through), **167 ms release** (recovers between kicks), +2.4 dB gain, Soft/R |
| 3 | Fruity Soft Clipper | On |
| 4 | Fruity Limiter | CEIL -1.0 dB |

## Tweak per song
- Kick and bass carry house: sidechain the bass (and pads) to the kick first.
- If the vocal feels far away, lower reverb wet before touching EQ.
- Deep house: drop the master compressor ratio toward 1.7:1. Tech/club house: push toward 3:1.
